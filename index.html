25 January

The First Time I Saw You

25 January ko school farewell tha.
Us din maine tumhe pehli baar properly notice kiya tha.

Tum white suit aur black goggles mein thi, aur tumhara dance bhi yaad hai. 🥹🤍
Sach bolu toh us waqt tum mujhe bahut cute lagi thi.

Mera mann tha tumse baat karne ka, lekin mere andar itni himmat nahi thi. Na tumhari ID thi, na number… bas tumhari woh first memory mere paas reh gayi.

14 February

We Found Each Other Again

Farewell ke baad mujhe bilkul nahi laga tha ki hum dobara kabhi milenge.

Mujhe laga tha shayad woh bas ek chhoti si memory thi jo wahi khatam ho gayi…

Lekin shayad Bhagwan bhi yahi chahte the ki hum dobara milein. 🥹🤍

Phir 14 February ko Instagram aur friends group ke through hum dobara connect hue.

Pehle bas normal si conversation hui… hi, hello, thodi baatein.

Dheere-dheere hum friends banne lage.

Tab mujhe bilkul idea nahi tha ki ek normal si conversation meri life ki itni important memory ban jayegi. 🫶🏻

17 February

The Day Everything Changed

17 February ki subah tumne mujhe personally “All the Best” bola tha.

Shayad tumhare liye woh ek simple si baat thi, lekin mere liye woh moment bahut special tha. 🤍

Phir usi din shaam ko mere friend ne tumhe bata diya ki main tumhe like karta hoon.

Maine apne friend ko pehle bola tha ki Subhana ko mat batana ki mujhe woh pasand hai…

Lekin usne meri baat nahi maani aur tumhe bata diya. 😭

Aur usi din shaam ko humari pehli baar call par baat hui thi.

Tumhari awaaz pehli baar call par sunna bhi mere liye ek alag hi memory thi. 🥹🎀

Usi time mujhe ye bhi pata chala ki tumne mera naam “best friend” ke naam se save kiya hua tha.

Phir friends ki wajah se humare beech relationship wali baat bhi aayi.

Aur 17 February ko tumne mujhe pehli baar “haan” bhi bola tha. 🥹🤍

Lekin agle hi din, 18 February ko kuch reason ki wajah se tumne mana kar diya tha.

Us waqt shayad mujhe khud nahi pata tha ki ye story itni door tak jaane wali hai.

4 March

The Rose

4 March ko maine school ke bahar tumhe rose diya.

Maine socha tha shayad ye moment filmy sa hoga… 🌹

Lekin tumne rose accept nahi kiya.

Us waqt thoda bura laga tha, lekin maine story ko wahi khatam nahi hone diya.

18 March

We Still Continued

18 March ke baad bhi humari story rukki nahi.

Aur 18 March mere liye humari story ki sabse important dates mein se ek ban gaya.

Us din hum Lake View ghoomne gaye the.

Wahin, us special moment mein, tumne mujhe dobara “haan” bola tha. 🥹🤍

Woh “haan” mere liye life-changing tha.

Us moment ko main kabhi nahi bhool sakta, kyunki us din mujhe laga tha ki shayad humari story ko ek naya beginning mil gaya hai.

Beech mein confusion, ups and downs aur bahut saari situations aayi…

Lekin somehow hum phir bhi connected rahe.

Aur shayad isi wajah se humari story mere liye aur special hoti gayi.

4 April

Another “Yes”

4 April ko humare beech ek aur important “yes” aaya.

Har baar ki tarah mujhe laga ki shayad ab sab kuch aur achha hoga.

Ye date bhi meri memories ka ek important part ban gayi.

21 May

Another Special Moment

21 May ko bhi humare beech ek important “yes” hua.

Humari story mein sirf ek hi type ke moments nahi the…

Kabhi happiness, kabhi fights, kabhi confusion, kabhi manaana — sab kuch tha.

Lekin har moment ne humari story mein kuch na kuch add kiya.

9 June

Your Birthday

9 June tumhara birthday tha.

Us din mera bas itna mann tha ki tum khush raho aur tumhare face par smile rahe.

Tumhare liye kuch karna, gift dena aur tumhe special feel karwana mujhe genuinely achha lagta tha. 🎂🤍

July

Little Gifts, Little Memories

July mein gifts, chhoti-chhoti efforts aur bahut saari memories bani.

Shayad ye moments duniya ke liye chhote hon…

Lekin mere liye ye sab tumhare saath judi hui memories hain.

Aur isi tarah pata hi nahi chala ki humari story kitni saari dates aur moments collect kar chuki thi. 🥹

September

When Things Got Difficult

September humare liye easy nahi tha.

Ek meeting ke baad relative ne hume dekh liya, aur baat family tak pahunch gayi.

Phir family ki taraf se questions, restrictions aur different religion ko lekar concerns aane lage.

Hum dono ke liye situation difficult ho gayi.

Jo cheez pehle sirf hum dono ki thi, uske beech suddenly family aur future ki problems aa gayi.

Lekin meri feelings aur memories mere liye real thi.

14 October

9 Months

14 October.

Ye date main kabhi galat nahi karna chahta.

Humare 9 months complete. 🥹🤍

25 January ko maine tumhe pehli baar dekha tha…

Aur phir ek-ek karke itni memories bani ki mujhe khud believe nahi hota ki ek first look se shuru hui story itni important ban gayi.

9 months mein sab kuch perfect nahi tha.

Lekin jo memories bani, woh mere liye priceless hain.

2032

The Dream

Aur phir aata hai mera sabse bada dream…

2032.

Main chahta hoon ki tab tak main apni life ko settle karun, apna future build karun, apna ghar banaun…

Aur jab waqt sahi ho, tab tumhare ghar walon ke saamne tumhara haath maangne ke layak banun. 🤍

Ye koi guarantee nahi, bas mera dream hai — ek aisa future jiske baare mein sochna mujhe achha lagta hai.

Mujhe nahi pata future exactly kaisa hoga…

Lekin meri taraf se tumhare saath judi ye saari memories hamesha special rahengi.
